04Featured research · MSc thesis

Trust, AI andthe people who use it.

Research

MSc Strategic Marketing · Cranfield University · 2026

Go-to-Market Strategy for Navatech’s Intelligence Layer

Trust and the adoption of AI-powered safety technology in construction.

My thesis studied how trust affects whether construction companies and site workers would adopt Navatech's AI safety product. I surveyed 43 people and found that overall trust, rather than any one factor alone, was what most influenced willingness to adopt. From that, I built a go-to-market strategy for Navatech focused on earning trust from both buyers and frontline workers.

43

people surveyed

Why it matters

Safety technology on a construction site has two audiences: the business that buys it and the people who use it every day. A product only works if both trust it, so the go-to-market has to speak to both.

  1. Question01

    Does trust shape whether construction companies and site workers would adopt an AI-powered safety product?

  2. Method02

    A survey of 43 respondents.

  3. Finding03

    Overall trust, rather than any single factor alone, most influenced willingness to adopt.

  4. Application04

    A go-to-market strategy built on earning trust from both organisational buyers and frontline workers.
